About Origins Africa

An events company, rooted in Africa.

Origins Africa (Pty) Ltd is an events & speaking company based in Somerset West, founded in 2018. We supply the people who make events work — masters of ceremony, event directors, conference facilitators and keynote speakers — and we direct programmes end to end so the day feels effortless to everyone in the room.

Founded and led by Mathew Makoni, our team keeps your programme on schedule, bridges speakers and audiences, moderates panels and runs seamless hybrid events — with the warmth and polish clients describe as authentic, professional and genuinely enjoyable.
